Brand Standards

Design Guidelines & Slogan

To maintain visual authority and consistency, observe the following parameters when deploying the Yekari Global brand assets.

🛡️ Clear Space Rule

Maintain clear isolation space equal to at least 50% of the emblem's height around all lockups. Never crowd the logo with adjacent graphics or typography.

📐 Minimum Sizing

Digital minimum size: 24px height for standalone icon; 36px height for complete lockup with typography. Print minimum: 12mm width.

✨ Official Slogan

The mandatory corporate slogan is "BUILT FOR MORE". Typeset in all-caps Plus Jakarta Sans Bold with 0.12em letter spacing.

🔠 Official Typography

Primary Headings: Plus Jakarta Sans (Weights: 600, 700, 800). Technical & Numerical: JetBrains Mono (Weights: 500, 600).
